- **Four theoretical models are available** and the account picks which one it
  measures with. All questionnaire configuration stays inside the chosen model,
  never mixed.
- **Switching models breaks the historical series, and now you can see it.** The
  point where you switched is marked inside the chart, with the name of the
  incoming model: an unmarked jump reads exactly like a hidden data point, and
  they are different things.
- Every study records which model it was measured with, so the history stays
  complete without anyone writing it down.
