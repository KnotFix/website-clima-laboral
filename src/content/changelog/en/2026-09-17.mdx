- **Signing in can ask for a second factor.** Under My account → Security → Add two-step
  verification you link an authenticator application (Google Authenticator, Authy,
  1Password), and from then on signing in asks for six digits on top of the password. Your
  phone computes that number: it does not arrive by SMS or email.
- **With backup codes, so losing the phone is not losing the account.** Turning it on
  shows a list of single-use codes; keep them somewhere other than that phone.
- **Each person turns it on.** It is not imposed on anyone in the team, and whoever
  answers the survey never sees it: they still come in through their link or the kiosk,
  with no account.
