- **Define the statuses your roster actually uses.** Under Configuration → Payroll →
  Statuses you can rename the ones that come with the account, retire them and add
  others, like «Unpaid leave». Each one carries a checkbox, «Can receive surveys», that
  decides whether its people take part in studies and count toward your plan's people
  cap. A new status is born without the checkbox: nobody receives a survey until you
  decide it.
- **The minimum tenure to answer is now configurable.** It was a fixed 3 months; it
  lives under Configuration → General, between 0 and 60 months, and applies to the
  studies you set up from then on.
