  title: "Climate and Satisfaction separately, and uncapped invitations",
};

- **A study can be split in two.** A study that measures only Climate and another that
  measures only Satisfaction, launched within the same period, count as a single study in
  your plan. It lets you measure at two moments of the year with shorter surveys. Two
  studies of the same index are still two studies. See [Account and plan](/en/docs/account-and-plan).
- **The meter tells you when one half is still available.** With the first part
  launched, the plan screen no longer says you reached the limit: it says which index you
  can still measure without using another study. The notice on launch does the same.
- **The credit of a launched study is not returned.** Deleting a study that was already
  launched does not give the study back to your plan, and the delete dialog says so
  beforehand. A draft is deleted at no cost.
- **Email and WhatsApp invitations are no longer capped.** On any plan. What changes from
  one plan to another is how many people you measure and how many studies you launch.
- **Reminders, with a wait.** Sending the invitation to a branch again reaches only those
  who have not answered yet. The same person does not get it again on the same channel
  until the next day (about 20 hours), and the screen tells you from what time you can
  resend. Whoever has already answered receives nothing more. Each person gets at most
  the invitation and 3 reminders per study on each channel, email and WhatsApp. See
  [Announcing the survey](/en/docs/announcing).
