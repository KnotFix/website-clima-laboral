- **Cancelling no longer means writing to us.** It lives in My account → Plan
  and subscription, and it states up front what happens: everything keeps
  working until the last day of the period you already paid for, and after that
  the account becomes read-only with your results still available.
- **People answering a survey now see a privacy notice** before the first
  question, on kiosk and by email alike. It comes from the text you configure in
  Configuration → General, with a ready-made model to adapt.
- **Legal documents are accepted on entry.** If you were invited to an account
  —or if we update one— you are asked once, with the link to read them.
