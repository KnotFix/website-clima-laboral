- **The org chart is no longer just for looking.** In Configuration → Structure, with the
  «Org chart» switch on, the «+» under each card hangs a new branch from it. If that area
  already has people, it subdivides it: it creates a branch underneath and moves its people
  there. The card's «⋯» edits or deletes it. Reordering and moving branches still happens
  in the table. See [The four steps before your first study](/en/docs/first-study).
- **The confidentiality threshold never goes below 3.** With two people, the group's
  result hands each of them the exact answer of the other. Accounts that had it at 2 move
  to 3, and studies already run keep the threshold they were measured with. See
  [Anonymity and minimum N](/en/docs/anonymity).
- **Everything hidden explains why.** Next to every hidden result there is a «Why?» link
  with the two possible causes: fewer people answered than the threshold, or showing it
  would let someone work out another small group by subtraction. It does not say which
  of the two applies, because that would also reveal something about the group. Notices
  now read «Hidden for anonymity».
- **The history shows each figure with its filter.** When you change branch, years or
  index in Statistics, the screen waits for the new data instead of leaving the previous
  figures under the new filter. An index missing categories for anonymity is marked as
  partial, an empty series says why (no finished studies, that branch was not measured,
  or the year range leaves them out) and the goal line no longer joins two different
  theoretical models. See [The historical series](/en/docs/history).
