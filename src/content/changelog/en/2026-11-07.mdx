- **The org chart is no longer just for looking.** In Configuration → Structure, with the
  «Org chart» switch on, the «+» under each card hangs a new branch from it. If that area
  already has people, it subdivides it: it creates a branch underneath and moves its people
  there. The card's «⋯» edits or deletes it. Reordering and moving branches still happens
  in the table. See [The four steps before your first study](/en/docs/first-study).
