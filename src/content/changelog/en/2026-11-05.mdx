- **An assistant organizes your spreadsheet.** If your file does not follow the template,
  «Sort with the assistant» reads it with artificial intelligence and proposes which
  column holds each piece of data and which branch of your structure each person belongs
  to, including branches that do not exist yet. You see it as a tree before importing and
  fix whatever needs fixing; nothing is created until you click «Import». See
  [The roster spreadsheet](/en/docs/roster-format).
- **Importing in four steps.** File, Columns, Review and Confirm: you drag the file in
  and the rest shows up one thing at a time. The «Add» and «Sync» modes become a single
  checkbox, «Deactivate people who are not in the file», off unless you check it.
- **Organizing a roster that was already loaded.** If you imported everyone without a
  structure, running the same file through the assistant spreads them into their branches
  without duplicating anyone.
- **Nobody changes branch in the middle of a study.** While a study is collecting
  answers, the import does not move anyone out of a branch that has answers: it would
  split its results in two.
- **Two more providers in the legal documents.** Anthropic, which makes Claude, reads the
  roster file when you use the assistant, and Meta delivers the WhatsApp invitations. They
  are in the [Privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy) and the
  [Data processing addendum](/en/legal/dpa), which move to a new version: you will be
  asked to accept them again when you sign in.
