- **Your own Satisfaction theory.** Under Settings → Survey forms → Custom theories you
  write your categories and questions, decide how much each one weighs and which scale
  it is answered on (from 1 to 3 up to 1 to 10). It works like any catalog model and
  freezes once a study launches it; to change it afterwards you duplicate it. See
  [Theoretical models](/en/docs/models).
- **You can measure only Satisfaction or only Climate.** Two switches at the top of
  Settings → Survey forms, like the eNPS one. A study that did not measure an index is a
  gap in the history, not a break, and targets keep chaining. See
  [Satisfaction and Climate](/en/docs/indices).
- **WhatsApp invitations.** The same single-use link as the email, sent from Censuma's
  number to one person or a whole branch. It needs the phone number in the roster and
  has its own allowance in the plan. See [Announcing the survey](/en/docs/announcing).
- **Phone numbers from any country.** The organization has a country, used for any
  number written without `+`, and every number is checked against its country's rules.
  See [The roster spreadsheet](/en/docs/roster-format).
- **The kiosk works without signal.** The tablet stores answers on the device and
  uploads them on its own when the connection returns. You prepare it from the panel
  with a PIN. See [Running the survey: the kiosk](/en/docs/kiosk).
- **Demographic filters can no longer isolate a person.** When the study is finished,
  responses are split into groups of at least N people, and a filter by position, sex,
  age or tenure publishes only whole groups. When it cannot include the entire filter,
  the result says so with the «Partial coverage» label. See
  [Anonymity and minimum N](/en/docs/anonymity).
- **Easier to read.** «By question» is browsed one section at a time, the history charts
  carry a bar to pick which stretch to look at, and downloadable reports look like the
  screen.
