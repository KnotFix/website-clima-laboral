  title: "Going over the people limit no longer blocks you",
};

- **People above what your plan includes don't block anything: they are billed
  per head.** The plan screen tells you how many are over and what they add up
  to, and announces the rate before you go over, not after.
- **Email invitations have a per-period limit** and it is now actually enforced.
  If a large send doesn't fit, what fits goes out and the rest stays queued
  instead of being lost.
- **The custom plan can be requested from the pricing screen.** It existed
  before and appeared nowhere.
