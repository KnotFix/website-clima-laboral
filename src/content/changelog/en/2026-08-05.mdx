- **The PDF report draws**: bars, breakdowns and lines, with the same rules as
  the screen. It used to be a stack of number tables.
- **The steps of a scale are named with their words** —"Not at all", "Somewhat",
  "A lot"— in the legend and in the headers, across all three formats. They used
  to come out as bare numbers.
- **There is a single way to walk through questions** across the product: the
  same carousel, search and keyboard in a study and in the history.
