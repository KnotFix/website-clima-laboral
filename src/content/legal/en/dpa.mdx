This addendum governs Knotfix's processing of the personal data of the Client's roster and
of its employees' answers. **It forms part of the [Terms of service](/en/legal/terms) and
is incorporated into them by reference**: it is accepted when the contract is accepted and
requires no separate signature.

It is drafted under **Law No. 8968** and its implementing regulation, **Executive Decree
No. 37554-JP**.

## Each party's role

**The Client is the controller of the database.** It decides who is measured, what is
asked, what the results are used for and how long they are kept. It is responsible for
informing its employees and obtaining consent where applicable.

**Knotfix processes that data on the Client's behalf**, under its instructions, and not for
purposes of its own.

That split **does not release Knotfix**: article 11 of Law 8968 reaches anyone taking part
in any phase of processing, and the duty of secrecy survives the end of the relationship.

## Subject matter, duration and nature

| | |
|---|---|
| Subject matter | Providing the workplace climate survey service |
| Duration | That of the contract, plus the return and deletion periods in the final section |
| Nature | Collection, storage, statistical computation, aggregation, suppression and deletion |
| Purpose | Producing aggregated climate results for the Client |

## Data and data subjects

**Data subjects:** the Client's employees included in the scope of each study.

**Categories of data:**

- From the roster: name, job title, position in the organizational structure, and email
  where the survey is delivered that way.
- From participation: whether the person was selected and whether they have responded.
- From the answers: the values given and the segment attributes captured alongside them.

**Answers are not stored associated with the person.** Participation and answers live apart
with no link between them; the date is stored without a time, so the two ends cannot be
rejoined by order of arrival either. This is not a permission restriction: the link does
not exist in the database and therefore cannot be restored.

⚠️ **FREE-TEXT answers are delivered to the Client exactly as written**, along with the
branch of the organization they belong to and with no filtering of identifying details.
They remain unassociated with any person, but a text can identify whoever wrote it by its
content or its style, and the confidentiality threshold protects aggregates, not
individual records. If the Client uses open questions, that circumstance **must appear in
the notice it gives its employees**.

**Sensitive data:** the service is not designed to process it and the Client undertakes not
to configure it, per the [Terms](/en/legal/terms).

## Instructions

Knotfix processes the data solely under the Client's documented instructions, which are
this addendum, the contract, and the actions the Client performs inside the product.

If a Client instruction were to conflict with Law 8968, we tell the Client and do not carry
it out.

## Confidentiality

Knotfix staff with access to this data are bound by confidentiality that survives the end
of their employment. Access is granted on a need basis and is logged.

## Security

We apply the measures of article 10 of Law 8968, in particular: encryption in transit and
at rest, isolation of data by organization, access control by role, access logs, environment
separation, periodic backups, and periodic review of these measures.

## Anonymity as a technical measure

Beyond the above, Knotfix upholds two guarantees specific to the product:

1. **Structural separation.** Who took part and what was answered are stored with no link.
2. **Confidentiality threshold.** Results for groups below the threshold are not published,
   and the rule also applies where a hidden result could be deduced by subtracting visible
   ones.

**Neither can be switched off**, by Client configuration, by role, by support action, or at
the Client's express request. It is the condition for the measurement to be worth anything,
and that is why it is an obligation of Knotfix rather than an option of the Client.

## Sub-processors

The Client authorizes Knotfix to rely on the providers listed in the
[Privacy policy](/en/legal/privacy), with which we maintain contractual obligations
equivalent to those in this addendum.

Before adding a new one we give **30 days** notice. If the Client objects on
reasonable data protection grounds, it may terminate without penalty before the change takes
effect.

## International transfers

Part of the infrastructure sits outside Costa Rica. That transfer is made under article 14
of Law 8968, with the authorization the Client grants by accepting this addendum, and with
our commitment to verify that the destination offers an equivalent level of protection.

The Client represents that it has informed its employees of this.

## Data subject rights

If an employee exercises a right of access, rectification or deletion before Knotfix, **we
route it to the Client** —the controller— and assist with the technical means to handle it
**within the five business days** of article 7 of Law 8968.

For survey answers, that assistance has a structural limit: since the link between person
and answer does not exist, **it is not possible to locate, rectify or delete a specific
person's answer**. The Client understands and accepts this consequence, which is the flip
side of the anonymity guarantee.

## Security incidents

In the event of an incident affecting the Client's personal data, we notify the Client
**without undue delay and no later than 72 hours from detecting it**, stating what
happened, what data and people may be affected, what measures we took and which we
recommend. As controller, it falls to the Client to notify whoever is concerned.

## Return and deletion

Once the contract ends, the Client has **30 days** to export its information in a commonly
used format. After that, Knotfix **irreversibly deletes the data**, backups included,
within **90 days** of closure, except for what a legal obligation requires to be kept; in
that case what is kept is limited to the strictly necessary and remains protected by this
addendum.

On the Client's request we issue confirmation of deletion.

## Information and verification

On the Client's reasonable request we provide the information needed to demonstrate
compliance with this addendum.

The Client may also audit that compliance **once a year**, with **30 days** notice, during
business hours, over the systems that process its data and **at its own cost**. The audit
may not reach other clients' data or compromise the security of the service, and whoever
carries it out is bound by confidentiality. Where an incident affecting its data prompts
it, the audit may proceed without waiting for the yearly cadence or the full notice period.
