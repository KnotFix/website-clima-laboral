Clima has vocabulary of its own. This page defines it; the rest of the documentation
uses it assuming you have been here.

## Structure

**Node** — Each unit of the org tree: a country, a region, a store, a department. You
define the names and the depth.

**Root** — The node everything else hangs from. There is **exactly one** per
organization, and you create it: the account is not part of the tree.

**Leaf node** — A node with no children. The only place an employee can live.

**Subtree** — A node and everything below it. It is the unit of a study's scope and of
a manager's permission.

**Organization** — The paying customer: the data boundary. Nothing in one organization
is visible from another.

## Instrument

**Questionnaire** — Three sections: Satisfaction, Climate, and your own questions.

**Section 3** — The editable part of the questionnaire: the questions you write.

**Effective questionnaire** — The one a node *actually* gets: the one assigned to it,
or —if it has none— the one from the nearest ancestor that does.

**Theoretical model** — The framework used to measure one of the two fixed sections. It
defines the categories and, in Satisfaction, their weights. There is one per index.

**Anchor** — The word accompanying a scale value: «Nada», «Algo», «Mucho»,
«Completamente». They are part of the instrument and **are not translated**.

**Conditional question** — A question that only appears depending on how another was
answered.

**Series break** — What happens when a question changes what it asks: its old results
stop being comparable with the new ones and the historical line splits.

## Measurement

**Study** — One concrete measurement: a scope, an instrument and a period.

**Stratified sampling** — Choosing who gets asked by distributing the sample across
segments, rather than drawing names at random.

**Confidence level** — The parameter that determines sample size.

**Kiosk** — Responding from a tablet at the workplace, with several stations if needed.

**Participation** — How many people responded. Stored separately from the content of
the responses, and never crossed with it.

## Results

**Index** — The summary number for a section. There are two: Satisfaction and Climate.
**They are not compared against each other.**

**Satisfaction** — The index for how content people are with the aspects of their work.

**Climate** — The index for how the working environment is perceived as a whole.

**Category** — A dimension within an index. Which ones exist is defined by the model.

**Acceptable** — The grouping of responses on the favourable side of the scale. It is
what produces a question's **acceptance percentage**.

**Segment** — A slice of the results, crossing a branch with the demographic axes: sex,
age band and tenure band.

**Improvement target** — The next period's objective, computed per branch from its last
result and the rate you configured. It freezes when the study launches.

## Confidentiality

**Confidentiality threshold** (or **minimum N**) — How many responses a group needs
before its results can be published. One number per organization, set by the owner, and
**each study freezes the one in force when it was created**.

**Hidden result** — A result that is not published because its group falls below the
threshold, or because publishing it would allow one that does to be deduced. No role can
see it.

## Account

**Owner** — The commercial relationship. There is exactly one.

**Admin** — Configures the whole account.

**HR** (`rrhh`) — Operational role: roster and studies.

**Manager** (`gerente`) — Reads, does not write, and only their own branch.

**Roster** — The employee directory. It is administrative, not results: the
confidentiality threshold does not apply here.
