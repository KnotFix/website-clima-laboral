A survey that arrives with no warning gets poor participation. And participation is not
a vanity metric: **it is what decides how many results you will be able to publish**.
Every branch that does not gather enough responses gets hidden by the confidentiality
threshold — and there you lost the area, not the survey.

This page is material to use, not theory.

## The four things to say

**1. Why it is happening.** Not «because it is that time of year». If there is a
concrete reason —internal communication came out badly last year and something was done
about it— say it. People answer differently when they believe someone will read.

**2. That it is anonymous, and why they can believe it.** The most neglected part, and
the one that changes answers most. The word «anonymous» is not enough: you have to
explain the mechanism. See
[What to tell them about anonymity](/en/docs/employee-anonymity), which is written to
be forwarded.

**3. How long it takes and the deadline.** A concrete date. «Over the coming weeks»
mobilizes nobody.

**4. What happens afterwards.** The cheapest and most effective commitment: say when
results will be shared. And keep it — next year's participation depends almost entirely
on whether anything happened this time.

## How the invitation arrives

There are three routes, and they can be mixed in the same study, person by person:

- **Email.** Each person gets their own single-use link. It needs the email address in
  the roster.
- **WhatsApp.** The same single-use link, sent from Censuma's number with your
  organization's name. It needs the phone number in the roster (see
  [The roster spreadsheet](/en/docs/roster-format)). It usually reaches people better
  than email when they have no work email or do not read it.
- **Kiosk.** A tablet at the workplace, for people with neither email nor a phone at
  hand. See [Running the survey: the kiosk](/en/docs/kiosk).

Email and WhatsApp invitations are sent from the study's survey panel, to one person or
to a whole branch, and **only while the study is running**. The product records that
the invitation went out, but **not whether it was read**: neither email opens nor
WhatsApp read receipts are stored. Each channel has its own allowance in the plan; see
[Account and plan](/en/docs/account-and-plan).

## What not to do

**Do not send it by email alone if half your people do not use email.** If you have
operations on a floor, a shop or a plant, WhatsApp and the kiosk exist for that. A
survey that only reaches desk workers measures desk workers.

**Do not promise changes you will not be able to make.** It is worse than not asking:
next time, people already know.

**Do not communicate it from HR only.** When the announcement comes down through direct
management, participation goes up. When it arrives as an institutional email alone, it
reads as paperwork.

**Do not let managers push too hard.** A manager standing next to the kiosk is the
fastest way to ruin perceived anonymity, however impeccable the system is.

## A template

To adapt, not to copy verbatim:

> **We are measuring workplace climate, and we need you to take part.**
>
> From **[date]** to **[date]** you can answer a survey about what it is like to work
> here. It takes around **[X] minutes**.
>
> **It is genuinely anonymous.** The system records that you responded —so we know how
> many people took part— but it keeps no link between you and your answers. Nobody can
> see what you wrote: not your manager, not HR, not the leadership team. On top of that,
> results for small groups are not shown, precisely so nobody can be identified by
> elimination.
>
> **What it is for.** Results are read per area, so what comes out of this can be worked
> on where it belongs. We will share the results on **[date]** and tell you what was
> decided.
>
> **How to respond.** [You will get a link by email or WhatsApp · Tablets are available at [place]
> during [hours]].
>
> If you have questions about the survey, write to **[person]**.

## While it is running

Watch participation per branch, not the overall figure. The global number can look fine
while an entire area sits at zero — and that is exactly the one you are about to lose.

If a branch is lagging, a reminder helps. What does not help is that reminder coming
from its direct manager, person by person.
